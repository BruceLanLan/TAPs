Conclusion: no Critical / High / Medium findings, 1 Low, 6 Info, plus 4 candidates that were investigated and judged NOT-AN-ISSUE. **The full report (method, evidence, PoCs, logs) is a separate file, `AUDIT.md`, and is not in this repository**; below are its findings table as written, and how each finding was handled afterwards in this repository.

## Findings (as written)

| ID | Severity | One line | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-01 | Low | `executeBatch` keeps and returns all callee return data, so the holder's gas grows quadratically with the total returned, and the gas a relayer needs is not determined by the signed values | CONFIRMED (PoC) |
